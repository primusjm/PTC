Who we are

A therapeutic community, not an institution.

We are a fully registered, NACADA-accredited addiction recovery centre, known for breaking the cycle of addiction and restoring health to individuals, families and whole communities.

Every member of staff shares one belief: addiction is a treatable disease, and recovery heals the mind, body and spirit together. So treatment here is medical, psychological, spiritual and holistic — because we are treating a person, not a habit.

Your culture, your family and your values sit at the centre of your recovery. Our whole structure — the facility, the wholesome food, the staff — exists to make it safe for you to speak honestly.

Our vision A healthy society free from addictions.
Core values Confidentiality · Accountability · Empowerment · Participation

Is addiction a disease?

Yes. And that changes everything about how we treat you.

Repeated use floods the brain with dopamine. In time the brain stops making normal amounts on its own, so the substance is prioritised over everything that once mattered — and going without brings real physical pain. This is a chronic, treatable medical condition, not a failure of character.

Classified as a chronic brain disorder by the American Society of Addiction Medicine and the National Institute on Drug Abuse.
