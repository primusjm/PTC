What treatment actually looks like, day by day.

Nobody arrives knowing what to expect. This page walks through the whole of it — detox, residential care and the shape of an ordinary week — so you can picture it before you commit to anything.

Our philosophy

We treat you as someone with the capacity to recover.

The basis of our treatment is a belief about you: that a chemically dependent person has the capacity to abstain from mood-altering drugs, and the capacity to become who they were meant to be. Everything we do proceeds from that.

Our programmes focus on personal and spiritual growth in recovery. Success is not only abstinence — it is self-esteem returning, relationships and family life improving, a workable relationship with a power greater than yourself, productive work, and realistic life goals set and then actually reached.

Stage one · Detoxification

Getting the substance out, safely.

A few days to a few weeks, depending on the substance, the severity of dependence and the support around you.

Important

Detox on its own is not treatment. It addresses the physical dependence and nothing more. Without counselling, group work and a residential programme to follow, the psychological side of the addiction is left untouched — and that is the side that causes relapse.

Stage two · Residential treatment

You live here while you recover. That is the point.

Taking you out of your daily environment removes the stress and the triggers that keep the cycle turning, and gives you whole blocks of time to work on the causes rather than the symptoms.

A week here

Therapy, education and time of your own.

Individual therapy usually one to three times a week; group therapy most days. The routine is deliberate — structure is itself part of the treatment.
